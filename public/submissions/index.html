Submissions

What we're looking for, and how to send it.

Vestibule Quarterly is a magazine about thresholds and waiting rooms, and the moment before you act. We want writing and images made in that pause. If that sounds like your work, send it.

What we publish

  • Essays and criticism. About attention, habit, and the systems people get caught in.
  • Fiction and prose poems. Kept short. We like the strange and the unsettling.
  • Field notes and marginalia. From wherever you work. Footnotes welcome.
  • Images. Photography, drawing, and diagrams.

Issue One: Normal Mode

We're reading now for our first issue, Normal Mode. It's about defaults and habit: the state you keep returning to, and the moment it stops working. Send us work about control and how it fails.

What we're after

We'd rather read something precise and curious than something loud. We like work that respects the reader and doesn't over-explain itself.

The particulars

Length
Prose to ~4,000 words · poetry up to 5 pieces · images at print resolution.
Form
Paste text or share a link (Google Doc, PDF, portfolio). Simultaneous submissions are fine; just tell us.
Rights
First publication; rights revert to you on release. We pay contributors.
Reply
We read every submission and respond, usually within eight weeks.

Send us your work

Fill this out and we'll be in touch. It goes straight to our inbox. No third-party form, no account needed.
