Submissions

What we're looking for, and how to send it.

Vestibule Quarterly is a magazine of the antechamber — the work that happens in doorways, waiting rooms, and thresholds. We want writing and images made in the pause before the thing itself: the moment between stimulus and response, between the shadow and the lit room, between normal mode and the command not yet entered. If a piece lives in that in-between, we want to read it.

What we publish

  • Essays & criticism — on liminal spaces, attention, systems and the people caught in them, the small optimizations of a life.
  • Fiction & prose poems — short. We favor the uncanny, the initiatory, the quietly mythic over the merely strange.
  • Field notes & marginalia — from the terminal, the lab, the archive, the crossroads. Footnotes welcome; obsession encouraged.
  • Images — photography, drawing, diagrams, and things that resist the caption.

What we're after

Precision over volume. A clear eye. Curiosity that has done its reading. We like work that treats a mystery as something to enter rather than solve, and that trusts the reader to cross the threshold on their own. Subtlety travels further here than spectacle.

The particulars

Length
Prose to ~4,000 words · poetry up to 5 pieces · images at print resolution.
Form
Paste text or share a link (Google Doc, PDF, portfolio). Simultaneous submissions are fine — just tell us.
Rights
First publication; rights revert to you on release. We pay contributors.
Reply
We read every submission and respond, usually within eight weeks.
